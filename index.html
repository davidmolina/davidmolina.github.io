Hi, I’m David Molina.

I build systems for operators.

Most businesses don’t struggle because of effort — they struggle because they don’t have clarity.

My work focuses on helping contractors and operators understand their numbers, structure their work, and run more predictable operations.

Read the book Master Takeoff and 1:1 Coaching

What I’m focused on

  • Helping business owners value their business and exit to retirement
  • Writing The Principles of MESLO (2026)
  • Teaching operators how to price and run work profitably
  • Building tools that connect estimating, operations, and execution

The work

After my last active-duty assignment, I got into construction and federal contracting, where the real problem wasn’t getting jobs — it was running them profitably.

That led to building the MESLO framework: a system to structure costs, pricing, and operations in a way that reflects how work actually happens.

It’s now used by contractors, home inspectors, and service businesses to bring clarity to their numbers and their day-to-day operations. The objective: a profitable business that’s predictable and valued more for a business owner's exit to retirement. Full bio and background on the about page, or resume.
