I’m David Molina—an author, entrepreneur, and M&A advisor. My work focuses on the systems behind durable organizations: how they price, decide, operate, grow, and exit.

My perspective has been shaped by military leadership, entrepreneurship, federal contracting, nonprofit leadership, board service, advising business owners, and building the MESLO framework.
