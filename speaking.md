      <h3>Know the Opportunity. Know Your Number. Make the Decision.</h3>
      <h4>Modern Estimating &amp; Government Contracting</h4>
      <p>Government contracting creates opportunity, but opportunity alone does not create profitable businesses.</p>
      <p>David Molina takes contractors inside the decisions that happen before a bid is submitted: understanding the solicitation, deciding whether to pursue it, building the estimate, knowing the company's true costs, pricing for risk and profit, and developing the operational systems necessary to perform.</p>
      <p>Drawing from <em>The Principles of MESLO</em>, federal contracting experience, and work with small-business technical assistance programs, this session connects modern estimating, AI-assisted solicitation analysis, go/no-go decision-making, and operational readiness into one practical system.</p>
      <p class="speaking-meta"><strong>Best for:</strong> APEX Accelerators · procurement technical-assistance programs · ports · transit agencies · DOT programs · SBA/resource partners · contractor associations · construction conferences · colleges and workforce programs</p>

    <h2 id="bio-heading">Short Bio</h2>
    <p>David Molina is an author, entrepreneur, M&amp;A advisor, and former U.S. Army Captain who speaks about the systems behind durable organizations. He is the author of <em>The Principles of MESLO: The Art &amp; Science of an Estimate</em> and founder of the MESLO framework. His experience spans military leadership, government contracting, entrepreneurship, technology, nonprofit leadership, and business transactions. His talks connect practical operating experience with a central question: how do we build organizations that become less dependent on their founders and more valuable over time?</p>

  (function () {
    var form = document.getElementById("speaking-inquiry-form");

    if (!form) {
      return;
    }

    form.addEventListener("submit", function (event) {
      event.preventDefault();

      var data = new FormData(form);
      var company = data.get("Company / Organization") || "Organization";
      var location = data.get("Event Location") || "Event Location";
      var attendance = data.get("Expected Attendance") || "Expected Attendance";
      var topic = data.get("Topic of Interest") || "Topic of Interest";
      var subject = "Speaking: " + company + " at " + location + " for " + attendance + " on " + topic;
      var lines = [];

      data.forEach(function (value, key) {
        lines.push(key + ": " + value);
      });

      window.location.href = "mailto:david@molinas.co?subject=" + encodeURIComponent(subject) + "&body=" + encodeURIComponent(lines.join("\n"));
    });
  })();
</script>
