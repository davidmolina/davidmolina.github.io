YAKIMA, Wash. — This week we kicked off a week-long book tour and signing in a part of the Pacific Northwest that often gets very little attention and news coverage-- eastern Washington. It's an area where you can traverse a long distance in an hour or two, witness deer crossings, rolling fields where the horizon meets, and mountainous terrain that exposes the most beautiful rock formations. 

Tonight, the Central Washington Home Builders Association (CWHBA) held their Día de los Muertos event with a live DJ, no-host bar, Mexican-style buffet, Loteria, and a raffle at Yakima-based, Puerto Escondido. Loteria was a real treat and brought back so many positive, familiar memories. Unlike bingo, a Loteria winner has to yell, "Loteria!" It was a very fun night that mixed business networking with the ambiance of one of the most iconic, Mexican competitive card games. 

Inspired by the contractor conversations, I gifted a copy of [The Principles of MESLO: the Art & Science of an Estimate](https://principlesofmeslo.com/) to the raffle table. Once it came to my donated book, the CWHBA president handed me the mic to say a few words about the donated copy and what it is. 

I got up, introduced myself, and told the crowd: 
> "I wrote this book after unexpectedly helping a woman-owned certified contractor win a state-government contract... after twelve months of consulting it became obvious that the MESLO estimating and business systems book needed to get into more hands." 

After my short remarks I reached into the bowl, selected and read the name out loud, and handed the winning raffle contractor his paperback copy. He was ecstatic. We sat down, I signed his copy, and we got talking. 

After the closing, several other contractors approached and wanted to meet and learn more about the book and the author behind it. I learned about local contracting challenges and the struggles of growing a business. After a quick chat, I sold and signed additional copies. 

I could not be more excited to share the MESLO pricing framework, the operational lessons learned, and digital best practices with CWHBA contractors in central Washington.
